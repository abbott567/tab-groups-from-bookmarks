# Privacy policy

Tab Groups from Bookmarks does not collect, send or share any data.

- It reads the nested folders inside your "Tab Groups" folders, so it can list them as groups and open their bookmarks.
- It only changes your bookmarks if you choose **set up using demo content**. This creates a "Tab Groups" folder on your bookmarks bar, with a demo folder and bookmarks inside of it.
- It saves the colour you choose for each group, and which open tab group belongs to which folder, in Chrome's storage on your computer.
- Nothing leaves your device. There are no analytics, no tracking and no servers.

Removing the extension deletes the colours it saved. It does not change your bookmarks, so your "Tab Groups" folders, including the demo folder, stay where they are.
